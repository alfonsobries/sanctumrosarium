A traditional church stained glass panel, seen lit from behind: jewel-toned glass in deep cobalt blue, ruby red, emerald green, amber and gold, joined by dark lead came; faces, hands and drapery painted on the glass in fine grisaille linework; light glowing through the colors. Neo-Gothic style, as in a nineteenth-century European cathedral window, with small Gothic tracery ornaments in the upper corners. Square composition, full bleed: the glass fills the whole image edge to edge, with no arched window outline, no black surround, no wall and no margin. Golden halos on the holy figures. No text, no letters, no signatures, no watermark. Catholic iconography, respectful and serene.
