Devotional painting in the tradition of New Spain colonial art (pintura novohispana), as painted in eighteenth-century Mexico: warm earthy pigments, soft modelling, gentle faces with dignity and reverence. Palette drawn from the tilma of Our Lady of Guadalupe: deep teal-green, rose and soft pink, warm gold and ochre, cream. Small gold stars and roses woven into the scene itself. Gold rays of light where heaven appears. Paint only the figures the scene names: no dove and no extra angels or cherubs unless the scene asks for them. Square composition, full bleed: the painted scene fills the whole canvas edge to edge, with no border, frame, margin or vignette, the main figures centered with breathing room. No text, no letters, no signatures, no watermark. Catholic iconography, respectful and serene.
