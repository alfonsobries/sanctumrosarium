Devotional painting in the tradition of New Spain colonial art (pintura novohispana), as painted in eighteenth-century Mexico: warm earthy pigments, soft modelling, gentle faces with dignity and reverence. Palette drawn from the tilma of Our Lady of Guadalupe: deep teal-green, rose and soft pink, warm gold and ochre, cream. A thin ornamental border of small gold stars and roses frames the scene. Gold rays of light where heaven appears. Portrait orientation, the scene centered with breathing room. No text, no letters, no signatures, no watermark. Catholic iconography, respectful and serene.
