A warm, gentle picture-book illustration for children: soft colored pencil and watercolor on cream paper, fine visible pencil texture, golden glowing light. Figures with kind, round, friendly faces and tender expressions, simple clear shapes a child can read at a glance; wherever the Virgin Mary appears she wears a soft blue mantle; delicate gold halos on the holy figures. Paint only the figures the scene names, nobody added to it. Palette of honey gold, warm ochre, sky and cornflower blue, soft red and cream. Square composition, full bleed: the scene and its background fill the whole image edge to edge, with no vignette, no fade to white, no border and no margin, the main figures centered. Nothing frightening: no blood, no wounds, no raised whips, no mocking faces; sorrowful scenes shown quietly, with tenderness and hope. No text, no letters, no signatures, no watermark. Catholic iconography, respectful and serene.
