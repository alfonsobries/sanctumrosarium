Sanctum Rosarium is for praying the Holy Rosary every day. The day's mystery is ready in one tap, and every prayer is taken from Church sources.

PRAY WITH A VOICE
Two voices pray with you, as in a community: one leads and the other answers. It keeps going with the screen off and answers your headphones.

AT YOUR OWN PACE
Move on with a tap or a swipe. The decade's beads always show where you are.

COMPLETE ON APPLE WATCH
The whole Rosary on your wrist, with its own voice, the words to follow along and a tap on every bead. The crown sets the volume.

THE GOSPEL OF EACH MYSTERY
Before each decade, the passage that tells it. When several evangelists tell the same mystery, each time reads a different one.

LEARN TO PRAY IT
Five short steps to begin, the twenty mysteries with their passage and every prayer with its source.

A QUIET JOURNAL
Remember the days you prayed. It counts rosaries and decades, with no streaks and no comparisons.

NINE STYLES
From the plainest to an illuminated manuscript, the old masters, Our Lady of Guadalupe and a large print edition. Light and dark in each.

FOUR LANGUAGES
Spanish from Mexico and from Spain, English and Latin, with a voice in all four.

WHAT YOU PRAY STAYS WITH YOU
It works with no account and no connection. No ads, no server and no analytics: your journal lives only on your phone.
